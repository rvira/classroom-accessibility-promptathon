# Bullseye Slide Deck · Speaker Guide

**Presentation Goal:** 3-minute pitch demonstrating how Bullseye eliminates cognitive paralysis for neurodivergent students using Now-First design.

---

### Slide 1: Cover · The Vision
- **What to say:** "Bullseye is a classroom accessibility layer built for students with ADHD and working-memory challenges. When attention drifts, it gives students an immediate, dignified route back into the lecture."
- **Key visual:** The clean live interface with its elevated center action card.

---

### Slide 2: The Drift · Before vs. After
- **What to say:** "A 60-second lapse in a fast-paced STEM lecture usually causes a total cognitive crash—students spend 20 minutes decoding slide walls and buried footnotes. Bullseye drops that recovery latency to under 10 seconds."
- **Key visual:** Chaotic slide wall (20m lost) vs. Bullseye Now-First card (<10s rejoin).

---

### Slide 3: Triadic Working-Memory Horizon · Cognitive Architecture
- **What to say:** "Our Triadic Working-Memory Horizon uses spatial depth projection: antecedent context anchors on the left, invariant LMS constraints lock on the right, and the focal active directive elevates forward in depth to eliminate attentional fragmentation."
- **Key visual:** Spatial 3D depth separating antecedent context, focal execution target, and invariant submission parameters.

---

### Slide 4: Real-Time Sync · How It Works
- **What to say:** "We integrate directly with Canvas LMS deadlines, EdStem student questions, and live classroom audio. Our engine breaks complex prompts into single verifiable steps with zero hallucination."
- **Key visual:** Clean 3-node pipeline: Campus Inputs ➔ Bullseye Engine ➔ Student Focus Screen.

---

### Slide 5: Dignity-First · Zero Stigma
- **What to say:** "Accommodations are built into the default workflow: pressing 'c' freezes motion into Calm Mode, the 'I'm stuck' flow offers neutral recovery without shame, and full keyboard navigation operates out of the box."
- **Key visual:** Three visual controls: Calm Mode toggle, neutral Rejoin card, keyboard keys.

---

### Slide 6: Impact & Next Steps
- **What to say:** "We cut orientation latency by 98% with zero diagnostic disclosure needed. The prototype is live, WCAG 2.2 AA verified, and ready for classroom pilots at UC Irvine."
- **Key visual:** Big stats (`<10s` Rejoin, `0` Stigma, `100%` Sync) and pilot launch badge.
